SunSchool Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

SunSchool is a school communication and management application that helps parents, teachers and school administrators access relevant school information and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how users can contact the school regarding their personal information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the user's role and use of the application, information may include:

  • Parent and teacher names and contact details.
  • Email addresses and mobile numbers.
  • Login and account information.
  • Student details needed for school administration.
  • Attendance, academic results, homework and related school records.
  • Fee records and payment status.
  • Profile photographs and teacher qualification certificates.
  • Documents attached to leave requests.
  • Optional Aadhaar number entered as text during registration.

SunSchool does not request or collect Aadhaar document photographs through the registration process.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Create and manage parent and teacher accounts.
  • Maintain student and school records.
  • Manage attendance, homework and academic information.
  • Display fee details and payment status.
  • Send relevant school announcements and notifications.
  • Process teacher registrations and leave requests.
  • Provide school-related support and communication.

4. Fees and Payments

The current version of SunSchool displays fee details, pending amounts and payment status. It does not currently provide an integrated online payment gateway or collect card or banking details for online payments.

5. Notifications

SunSchool uses push notifications and in-app notifications to communicate relevant school information, such as attendance updates and announcements.

SMS notifications are not currently implemented in the application.

6. Storage and Third-Party Services

SunSchool uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage and Firebase Cloud Messaging, to support account authentication, school data storage, uploaded documents and notifications.

Information processed by these services is subject to the applicable Google privacy terms and policies.

7. Children's Information

SunSchool may process student information as part of school administration. Student information is provided or managed through the school and associated parent or guardian accounts. The application is intended for school-related use.

8. Information Sharing

Information may be made available to authorized school personnel and relevant users where necessary for school operations. Information may also be processed by the service providers described in this policy to operate the application.

9. Data Security

The school and application operators should apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Personal information is retained as needed for school operations and applicable record-keeping requirements.

Users who want to request deletion of their SunSchool account and associated personal information can contact the school using the email address below. Please include the registered account email or mobile number and identify the account holder. The school may need to verify the request before processing it.

Certain records may need to be retained where required for legitimate school, legal or record-keeping purposes. Any such retention should be explained to the requester.
